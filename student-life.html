Student Life

Growing Through Learning, Leadership & Experience

Student life at Ndejje Central High School – Zanta extends beyond the classroom, giving learners opportunities to develop skills, confidence, responsibility, creativity and leadership.

Home / Student Life
Learn • Produce • Earn • Lead Student experiences are designed to connect knowledge with practical capability and responsible leadership.
Life Beyond Lessons

Education is also about who the learner becomes

We believe that education should help young people develop more than academic knowledge. Learners need opportunities to discover their interests, practise useful skills, work with others, take responsibility and develop confidence.

Student life therefore forms an important part of the wider educational experience at Ndejje Central High School – Zanta.

  • Leadership and responsibility
  • Teamwork and communication
  • Practical skills development
  • Innovation and creativity
  • Entrepreneurship and enterprise
  • Discipline and personal development
