About Our School

Education Beyond the Classroom

Ndejje Central High School – Zanta is a Centre of Knowledge committed to providing holistic education that develops the whole learner.

Our approach combines academic learning with discipline, innovation, entrepreneurship, technology and practical skills development.

We believe learners should not only prepare for examinations, but should also develop the confidence, competence and creativity needed to contribute meaningfully to society.

  • Academic Excellence
  • Character Development
  • Innovation & Technology
  • Practical Skills
  • Entrepreneurship
  • Leadership Development

ISIE Learning Journey

Our Training Model

Learners can progressively move from exploration to specialization and practical enterprise projects.

Foundation

S.1 – S.2

Students explore ICT, agriculture, entrepreneurship and vocational skills while building foundational competencies.

Exploration

S.3 – S.4

Students explore skill tracks and begin developing practical projects and enterprise ideas.

Specialization

S.5 – S.6

Students deepen their selected areas and participate in more advanced production, innovation and enterprise activities.
