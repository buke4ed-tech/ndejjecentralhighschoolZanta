Academic Approach

Building strong learners for a changing world

Our academic programme is designed to develop strong foundations in knowledge while giving learners opportunities to apply what they learn.

Through our Integrated Skills, Innovation & Enterprise approach, learners are encouraged to connect classroom learning with practical skills, problem-solving, creativity and enterprise.

  • Strong academic foundations
  • Practical and applied learning
  • ICT and digital skills development
  • Innovation and problem-solving
  • Entrepreneurship and enterprise
  • Leadership, discipline and responsibility

Assessment & Progress

Understanding Learner Progress

Assessment is an important part of the learning process, helping teachers and learners understand strengths, needs and areas for continued development.

Continuous Learning

Learning progress can be followed through classroom activities, exercises, assignments, projects and other academic work.

  • Classroom activities
  • Assignments and coursework
  • Practical activities
  • Projects and learner participation

Tests & Examinations

Formal assessments provide opportunities to measure understanding and review academic progress.

  • Subject assessments
  • Term-based examinations
  • Academic review
  • Performance feedback

Academic Support

Assessment information can help identify areas where learners require additional guidance, practice or support.

  • Identifying learning needs
  • Targeted academic support
  • Progress monitoring
  • Communication with learners and families

Results & Development

Integrated Skills, Innovation & Enterprise

Bringing Education to Life

Our ISIE approach helps connect academic learning with practical competencies, innovation and productive enterprise.

S

Skills

Learners develop practical competencies alongside academic knowledge, including digital, vocational, agricultural and creative skills.

I

Innovation

Learners are encouraged to think creatively, identify problems and develop useful solutions.

E

Enterprise

Enterprise learning develops initiative, financial awareness and the ability to turn knowledge and skills into productive opportunities.

Learner Development

The Academic & Skills Journey

Learners can progressively explore, develop and specialise their academic and practical capabilities.

Stage 1

Foundation

S.1 – S.2

Learners build academic foundations while exploring ICT, agriculture, entrepreneurship and practical skills.

Stage 2

Exploration

S.3 – S.4

Learners deepen their academic understanding, explore skill tracks and participate in practical and small-enterprise projects.

Stage 3

Specialisation

S.5 – S.6

Learners continue academic development while specialising in selected areas and engaging in production-based projects and enterprise opportunities.
