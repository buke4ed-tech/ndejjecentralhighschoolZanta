Discover who we are, what we believe in, how we educate, and how we prepare young people for academic success, responsible citizenship, innovation and enterprise.

Centre of Knowledge
Who We Are

Education for Knowledge, Character & Opportunity

Ndejje Central High School – Zanta is committed to providing learners with an education that develops academic knowledge, discipline, practical competence, creativity and responsible citizenship.

We believe that education should prepare young people not only for examinations, but also for life beyond school. Our approach therefore combines classroom learning with practical skills, innovation, entrepreneurship and opportunities for learners to discover and develop their talents.

Through our Integrated Skills, Innovation & Enterprise approach, we seek to create an environment where learners can learn, experiment, create, collaborate and develop solutions to real-world challenges.

  • Academic development and examination preparation
  • Character, discipline and responsible citizenship
  • Practical and vocational skills development
  • ICT, innovation and entrepreneurship
  • Talent development and student leadership
Our Direction

Vision & Mission

Our educational direction is built around academic development, character formation, practical competence and preparation for the future.

◉

Our Vision

To nurture competent, disciplined, innovative and morally responsible young people who are prepared to contribute positively to their families, communities and country.

◆

Our Mission

To provide quality and holistic education that develops academic competence, practical skills, creativity, discipline, entrepreneurship and responsible citizenship.

What Guides Us

Our Core Values

These values guide our approach to teaching, learning, leadership and relationships within the school community.

01

Excellence

We encourage learners and staff to pursue high standards in academic, practical and personal development.

02

Discipline

We promote responsibility, respect, self-control and conduct that supports a productive learning environment.

03

Innovation

We encourage curiosity, creativity, problem-solving and the development of practical solutions.

04

Integrity

We value honesty, responsibility, respect and ethical behaviour in school and beyond.

Our Distinctive Approach

Integrated Skills, Innovation & Enterprise

The ISIE approach complements academic education with practical learning and opportunities for learners to develop useful skills, explore ideas and understand enterprise.

The objective is to help learners connect what they learn in class with practical applications and real-world challenges.

  • Digital Skills & ICT
  • Entrepreneurship
  • Smart Agribusiness
  • Vocational Skills
  • Creative Industries
  • Environmental Innovation
How We Educate

Our Educational Philosophy

We aim to develop the whole learner by connecting knowledge, character, skills and practical experience.

01

Learn

Learners develop strong foundations through classroom instruction, guided study, research and academic engagement.

02

Practise

Learners are encouraged to apply knowledge through projects, practical activities, experimentation, teamwork and skills-based learning.

03

Create & Lead

Learners develop confidence, creativity, leadership, entrepreneurship and the ability to contribute to their communities.

School Leadership

Leadership

Headteacher
